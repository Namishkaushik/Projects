# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard
 
## 📌 Project Overview
 
This project analyzes the Superstore sales dataset using Microsoft Excel and Power BI to identify business trends, sales performance, profitability, and customer segments.
 
The objective was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, Pivot Tables, dashboards, and visualizations.
 
---
 
# 🛠 Tools Used
 
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Pivot Tables
- Charts & Visualizations
---
 
# 📂 Dataset
 
The dataset contains information about:
 
- Order ID, Order Date, Ship Date
- Ship Mode
- Customer ID, Customer Name
- Segment
- Country, City, State, Region
- Product ID, Category, Sub-Category, Product Name
- Sales
- Quantity
- Profit
- Returns
- Payment Mode

# 🔍 Business Insights
 
## 1. Category with Highest Sales
 
Office Supplies generated the highest sales:
 
- **Office Supplies:** $643,708
- **Technology:** $470,588
- **Furniture:** $451,509
### Recommendation
 
Focus inventory planning and marketing investments on Office Supplies products to maintain revenue growth.
 
---
 
## 2. Most Profitable Category
 
Technology produced the highest profit.
 
| Category | Profit |
|----------|--------:|
| Technology | $90,458 |
| Office Supplies | $74,797 |
| Furniture | $10,007 |
 
### Recommendation
 
Review pricing, supplier costs, and margins for Furniture products, which lag well behind the other two categories on profitability despite solid sales volume.
 
---
 
## 3. Highest Profit Sub-Categories
 
| Sub-Category | Profit |
|--------------|--------:|
| Copiers | $42,775 |
| Accessories | $25,337 |
| Phones | $22,309 |
 
### Recommendation
 
Increase inventory availability and marketing for these high-performing products.
 
---
 
## 4. Loss-Making Sub-Categories
 
| Sub-Category | Profit |
|--------------|--------:|
| Tables | -$11,092 |
| Supplies | -$1,654 |
| Bookcases | -$343 |
 
### Recommendation
 
Review pricing strategy and supplier costs for Tables in particular, since it is the single biggest drag on overall profitability.
 
---
 
## 5. Regional Performance
 
| Region | Sales | Profit |
|---------|-------:|--------:|
| West | $522,441 | $67,860 |
| East | $450,235 | $53,400 |
| Central | $341,008 | $27,450 |
| South | $252,121 | $26,552 |
 
### Recommendation
 
Replicate successful sales strategies from the West region in other regions.
 
---
 
## 6. Customer Segment Analysis
 
| Segment | Sales | Profit |
|---------|-------:|--------:|
| Consumer | $753,002 | $81,339 |
| Corporate | $509,743 | $57,806 |
| Home Office | $303,059 | $36,118 |
 
### Recommendation
 
The Consumer segment contributes nearly half of total sales, making it the primary customer base for targeted marketing campaigns.
 
---
 
## 7. Payment Mode Analysis
 
| Payment Mode | Sales | Profit |
|--------------|-------:|--------:|
| COD | $667,418 | $82,092 |
| Online | $553,993 | $54,048 |
| Cards | $344,393 | $39,122 |
 
Cash on Delivery (COD) is the dominant payment mode, accounting for over 42% of total sales, followed by Online payments and Cards.
 
### Recommendation
 
Ensure COD logistics and collection processes are well supported, since it drives the largest share of both sales and profit. Consider incentivizing Card and Online payments to reduce COD-related handling costs.

# 📌 Conclusion
 
This project demonstrates how Excel and Power BI can be used together to convert transactional sales data into actionable business insights.
 
The analysis highlights:
 
- Strong sales performance of Office Supplies, with Technology as the most profitable category
- Weak profitability within Furniture, driven largely by losses in Tables
- High-performing Consumer segment
- West region as the best-performing market
- COD as the leading payment mode by both sales and profit
These findings can support better inventory planning, pricing strategies, regional expansion, and marketing decisions.
