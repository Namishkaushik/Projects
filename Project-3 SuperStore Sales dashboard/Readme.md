# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore dataset using Microsoft Excel and Power BI to identify business trends, sales performance, profitability, and customer segments.

The objective was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, Pivot Tables, dashboards, and visualizations.

---

# 🛠 Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Pivot Tables
- Charts & Visualizations

# 🔍 Business Insights

## 1. Category with Highest Sales

Technology generated the highest sales:

- **Technology:** $836,154
- **Furniture:** $742,000
- **Office Supplies:** $719,047

### Recommendation

Focus inventory planning and marketing investments on Technology products to maintain revenue growth.

---

## 2. Most Profitable Category

Technology produced the highest profit.

| Category | Profit |
|----------|--------:|
| Technology | $145,455 |
| Office Supplies | $122,491 |
| Furniture | $18,451 |

### Recommendation

Review pricing, supplier costs, and discount strategy for Furniture products.

---

## 3. Highest Profit Sub-Categories

| Sub-Category | Profit |
|--------------|--------:|
| Copiers | $55,618 |
| Phones | $44,516 |
| Accessories | $41,937 |

### Recommendation

Increase inventory availability and marketing for these high-performing products.

---

## 4. Loss-Making Sub-Categories

| Sub-Category | Profit |
|--------------|--------:|
| Tables | -$17,725 |
| Bookcases | -$3,473 |
| Supplies | -$1,189 |

### Recommendation

Review discounts, pricing strategy, and supplier costs to reduce losses.

---

## 5. Regional Performance

| Region | Sales | Profit |
|---------|-------:|--------:|
| West | $725,458 | $108,418 |
| East | $678,781 | $91,523 |
| Central | $501,240 | $39,706 |
| South | $391,722 | $46,749 |

### Recommendation

Replicate successful sales strategies from the West region in other regions.

---

## 6. Customer Segment Analysis

| Segment | Sales | Profit |
|---------|-------:|--------:|
| Consumer | $1,161,401 | $134,119 |
| Corporate | $706,146 | $91,979 |
| Home Office | $429,653 | $60,299 |

### Recommendation

The Consumer segment contributes over half of total sales, making it the primary customer base for targeted marketing campaigns.

---

## 7. Discount Analysis

Higher discounts generally reduced profitability.

Discounts of **30% and above** frequently resulted in negative profits, while products sold with **0% discount** generated the highest overall profit.

### Recommendation

Avoid excessive discounting unless supported by strong sales volume or strategic objectives.

# 📌 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Excel and Power BI can be used together to convert transactional sales data into actionable business insights.

The analysis highlights:

- Strong performance of Technology products
- Weak profitability within Furniture
- High-performing Consumer segment
- West region as the best-performing market
- Negative impact of excessive discounts on profitability

These findings can support better inventory planning, pricing strategies, regional expansion, and marketing decisions.
